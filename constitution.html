Purpose and principles

Celesta is a student-led astronomy organization built around education, research, technical projects, outreach and hands-on STEM learning. This constitution explains how we work together and what members can expect from the organization and from each other.

  1. Celesta exists to make astronomy approachable without reducing its intellectual depth.
  2. Astronomy is our entry point to a wider range of science and technology, including data science, software, research methods and engineering.
  3. Celesta is student-led. Leaders and members are students who volunteer their time.
  4. We value access. Opportunities and learning material should reach students wherever they are.
  5. We share credit openly and name the sources, collaborators and mentors behind our work.

Core values

Six values guide how we learn, build and treat each other.

Curiosity

We ask questions first and treat not knowing yet as the start of learning.

Access

Astronomy and STEM opportunities should reach students beyond a conventional classroom.

Collaboration

We learn faster together, across schools, countries and levels of experience.

Integrity

We are honest about what we know, what we have done and where our ideas come from.

Rigor

We keep the depth in astronomy, check our work and welcome correction.

Service

We give our time to help others learn, and we follow through on what we commit to.

Membership

Celesta has three membership levels. The badge beside a name on the Members page shows which one it is.

  1. Leads are students who lead a team or the organization.
  2. Members are students who contribute to Celesta's projects, events and community.
  3. Interns are students who join through the internship program and work on technical, research, social media or organizational projects.
  4. Anyone with a passion to volunteer may apply for an internship. There are no other restrictions.
  5. Club IDs are given to members and open members-only pages. Please do not share yours outside Celesta.
  6. A member may step back at any time by letting a lead know.

Roles and responsibilities

Each role carries responsibilities, not just a title.

  1. The President guides Celesta's direction, represents the organization externally and chairs leadership meetings.
  2. The Joint Vice Presidents support the President, coordinate teams and step in when the President is unavailable.
  3. The Technical Lead looks after Celesta's technical work, including the website and software projects.
  4. The Social Media Leads manage Celesta's public channels and keep what we post accurate, respectful and consistent.
  5. Every lead keeps their team informed and hands their work over clearly when their role ends.

Rules and conduct

These rules apply on Discord, at workshops and events, and anywhere else people represent Celesta.

  1. Treat everyone with respect. Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated.
  2. Keep discussions constructive. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
  3. Be honest in academic and competition work. Credit your sources and never present someone else's work as your own.
  4. Protect privacy. Do not share personal information or private messages without permission.
  5. Use Celesta's name, logo and channels only for approved activities.
  6. Report concerns to a lead. Reports are handled fairly and with care.
  7. Serious or repeated breaches may lead to a warning, removal from a role or removal from Celesta.

Guidelines

Do

  • Ask for help and share what you learn.
  • Give feedback kindly and specifically.
  • Cite your sources.
  • Tell a lead early if you cannot finish something.

Avoid

  • Spam or unrelated promotion.
  • Sharing other people's private information.
  • Speaking for Celesta without approval.
  • Copying work without credit.

Decisions and amendments

How decisions are made, and how this constitution can change.

  1. Day-to-day decisions are made by the leads responsible for that area.
  2. Decisions that affect the whole organization are made by the leadership team by majority, after hearing from members.
  3. Important decisions and changes are announced to members on Discord.
  4. Any member can propose an amendment to this constitution by contacting a lead.
  5. An amendment passes when a majority of the leadership team approves it. The version number and date at the top are then updated.
